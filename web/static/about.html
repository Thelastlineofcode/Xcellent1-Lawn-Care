About Xcellent1 Lawn Care

Locally owned and operated — serving the River Parishes with professional lawn care since 2023.

Our Story

Xcellent1 Lawn Care was founded by La Cardio Francis — a local LaPlace, Louisiana resident who saw the need for reliable, professional lawn care in the River Parishes. What started as a small operation grew into a trusted local business serving homeowners and commercial properties across St. John the Baptist and St. Charles Parishes.

La Cardio built Xcellent1 on a simple principle: show up on time, do the job right, and treat every lawn like it's your own. That commitment to quality and consistency is what keeps customers coming back — and what sets Xcellent1 apart in a market where reliability is everything.

Our Mission

To provide dependable, high-quality lawn care to the River Parishes community — giving homeowners and businesses more time for what matters while keeping their property looking its best. Every job gets the same attention to detail, whether it's a small residential lot or a large commercial property.

The Owner's Vision

La Cardio's goal is to build a self-regulating business — one where systems and accountability do the work, not just the owner. He envisions a crew that knows their performance before anyone tells them, jobs that run on clear procedures rather than phone calls, and a business that delivers quality without the owner having to watch every detail.

As he puts it: "I ain't got to tell you your performance because the app gonna tell you. The app got you in red."

That drive to build a better, more reliable system is what fuels Xcellent1's growth — and it's the same mindset that ensures every customer gets consistent, professional service every time.
