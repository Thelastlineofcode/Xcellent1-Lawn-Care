Weekly Lawn Maintenance

Precision mowing, edging, trimming, and blowing on a scheduled weekly or biweekly plan. We cut at the right height for your grass type — St. Augustine, Bermuda, or Zoysia — and keep your property looking sharp all season. Every visit includes clean edging along sidewalks and driveways, string trimming around fences and beds, and a full cleanup so nothing's left behind.

Edging & Trimming

Crisp, clean edges that separate your lawn from walkways, driveways, and flower beds. We edge every 2–3 mowings to keep borders sharp and prevent grass from creeping into mulch or cracks. Paired with precision string trimming around trees, fences, posts, and structures — the detail work that makes a lawn look professionally maintained.

Includes hedge trimming for shrubs and low-hanging branches.

Overgrown Lot Clearing

Full property clearing for lots that have been neglected — thick brush, vines, invasive weeds, debris, and small trees. We cut everything down, haul it away, and grade the lot if needed.
