How to Prepare Your Lawn for Hurricane Weather

What to do before the storm, what to avoid during cleanup, and how to help your grass recover.

July 26, 2026 · 7 min read

Hurricane weather can leave a River Parishes lawn covered in branches, packed down by standing water, and stressed by salt or contaminated floodwater. A little preparation reduces preventable damage and makes cleanup safer.

Before the storm

  • Mow slightly higher than usual so the lawn keeps leaf area and does not scalp when wet.
  • Clear loose planters, toys, hoses, tools, and decorations that can become projectiles.
  • Clean gutters, downspouts, and nearby drains so water has a path away from the house.
  • Photograph the lawn, fences, trees, and irrigation equipment for your records.

Do not fertilize right before a major storm

Heavy rain can wash unused nutrients into drainage systems instead of into the root zone. Wait until the lawn has drained and you can assess the damage. A soil test is more useful than guessing at a fertilizer blend after a storm.

Safe cleanup after the storm

Wear gloves and closed-toe shoes, watch for downed lines, and leave large or damaged trees to qualified professionals. Remove light debris in stages rather than dragging heavy piles across wet turf. Raking saturated soil can tear roots and create ruts.

Helping the lawn recover

  • Give standing water time to drain before driving equipment over the area.
  • Pick up leaves and branches so the grass can breathe and sunlight can reach the blades.
  • Rinse visible salt or residue from foliage only when local water restrictions allow.
  • Wait for new growth before mowing again, then return gradually to your normal height.
  • Repair drainage problems before replacing dead sod or reseeding.

When to call for help

Call for an assessment when water remains for more than a day, a tree has shifted, soil has washed away, or debris is too heavy to move safely. Early grading and drainage work can prevent a temporary storm problem from becoming a recurring lawn failure.
